About the Conference

🔬

CONIAPS-XXXIII

The 33rd International Conference of the International Academy of Physical Sciences (CONIAPS-XXXIII) on Science, Technology and Interdisciplinary Innovations for Sustainable Development (STIISD-2027) brings together leading academicians, researchers, industry experts and young scientists from across the globe to share recent developments and innovative findings. It features keynote lectures, invited talks, oral and poster presentations.

🏛️

About IAPS

The International Academy of Physical Sciences (IAPS) was established on 4 December 1994 in Prayagraj, India. It is a distinguished scientific body devoted to advancing research, education and collaboration in the physical sciences — encompassing physics, chemistry, mathematics, computer science, earth sciences and related fields. IAPS organizes prestigious CONIAPS conferences and recognizes scientific excellence through awards and fellowships. www.iaps.org.in

🌱

About SHUATS

Sam Higginbottom University of Agriculture, Technology and Sciences (SHUATS) was established in 1910 in Prayagraj. Ranked 30th among all ICAR-State Agricultural Universities in India, it spans 900 acres with 300 acres of research farms. SHUATS supports scientific development across agricultural, environmental, and basic sciences with 17 constituent schools, 55 departments, and 4 advanced research centers serving over 10,000 students.

Submission Guidelines

Abstract / Paper Submission

  • Abstract: not exceeding 300 words with 5 keywords
  • Full paper: approximately 3,000 words
  • Format: MS Word, double-spaced, Times New Roman 12pt
  • First page must include: title, author name, affiliation, contact number and email
  • Best papers will be awarded
  • Submit to: coniaps33@shuats.ac.in

Poster Presentation

  • Physical presentation at the conference
  • Poster size: 48″ × 60″ (1.2–1.5 meters)
  • Flex printing recommended (good quality)
  • Physical presence of registered participant is mandatory during poster session
  • Certificate of participation for each presenter
  • Best posters will be selected for awards
📧 coniaps33@shuats.ac.in — Submit your abstract

Young Scientist Award (YSA)

Recognizing excellence in research by young scientists under 35

🏆 Young Scientist Award — CONIAPS-XXXIII

  • Awards given in Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, Computer Science, and Allied Sciences
  • YSA carries a certificate, cash prize and other benefits
  • Candidate must not have crossed 35 years of age on February 16, 2027 (as evidenced from Class X certificate)
  • Submit full-length paper along with age proof and registration fee
  • Number of authors in a YSA paper: not more than two
  • If second author is not the supervisor, a No Objection Certificate is required
  • Full paper deadline: February 10, 2027
  • Selected papers will be published in the Journal of International Academy of Physical Sciences (ISSN 0974-9373)
  • Submit CV and paper to: coniaps33@shuats.ac.in

Nearby Visiting Places

Prayagraj is at the heart of India's heritage and spiritual tourism

Anand Bhawan, Prayagraj

Historic ancestral home of the Nehru family, now a national museum. A symbol of India's freedom struggle — located in the heart of Prayagraj.

Triveni Sangam, Prayagraj

The sacred confluence of Ganga, Yamuna and the mythical Saraswati rivers. One of the holiest pilgrimage sites in Hinduism, host of the grand Kumbh Mela.

Bade Hanuman Ji Temple, Prayagraj

Famous for its unique reclining idol of Lord Hanuman. Located near Sangam, this temple is a must-visit spiritual landmark during Kumbh Mela.

Vindhyachal Temple, Mirzapur

One of the most sacred Shakti Peethas dedicated to Goddess Vindhyavasini. Located 70 km from Prayagraj on the banks of the Ganga in Mirzapur.

Varanasi

The Spiritual Capital of India — 120 km from Prayagraj. One of the world's oldest continuously inhabited cities.

Chitrakoot

The Land of Lord Rama — 130 km. A sacred town nestled in the Vindhya hills, associated with Ram's exile.

Ayodhya

Birthplace of Lord Rama — 170 km. One of the seven most sacred cities (Sapta Puri) in Hinduism.

Kaushambi

A Hidden Buddhist Heritage Site — 60 km. Ancient capital of the Vatsa kingdom with Ashoka's pillar.

Shringverpur

A Ramayana Connection — 40 km. Mythological crossing point of Lord Ram across the Ganga river.
